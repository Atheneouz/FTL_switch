======================================================================
  FTL: Faster Than Light - Nintendo Switch Port (NSP Packages)
======================================================================

In this folder you will find the official Forwarder ready to install
on your Nintendo Switch running Custom Firmware (Atmosphère):

1. FTL_Forwarder_[0100F71016210000].nsp (INCLUDED & READY)
   ----------------------------------------------------------
   - Type: Native HOME Menu shortcut to the MicroSD card.
   - Size: ~0.4 MB (free of commercial assets, legal to distribute).
   - Directly executes the game located at:
       sdmc:/switch/ftl/ftl.nro
   - Loads the iOS Mach-O binary 'FTL' and assets from 'FTL.app' on SD.
   - Game saves are preserved automatically in 'sdmc:/switch/ftl/save/'.
   - Allows updating the ftl.nro binary without reinstalling the game.

2. FTL_Standalone_RomFS_[0100F71016210000].nsp (OPTIONAL)
   ----------------------------------------------------------
   - Type: Native Standalone package (NSO in ExeFS + full RomFS).
   - Size: ~246 MB.
   - Due to Subset Games copyright, this package is not precompiled
     with game assets in this repository.
   - You can build it in a single command by running:
       python tools/build_nsp.py
     (after placing your .ipa file into the 'ipa/' folder).

======================================================================
  INSTALLATION STEPS ON YOUR NINTENDO SWITCH
======================================================================

1. Prepare game files on your MicroSD:
   - Place your 'FTL_xxx.ipa' file into the 'ipa/' folder.
   - Run:
       python tools/prepare_sd_card.py
   - Copy the 'switch' folder from 'sd_card/' to the root of your MicroSD.

2. Install the Forwarder on your console:
   - Connect the Switch to your PC via USB-C cable.
   - Open DBI on your Switch and select "Run MTP Responder".
   - On your PC, navigate to the Switch drive -> "5: MicroSD install" (or "4: NAND install").
   - Copy the file 'FTL_Forwarder_[0100F71016210000].nsp'.

3. Press the HOME button on your Switch and you will see the
   "FTL: Faster Than Light" icon ready to launch.
